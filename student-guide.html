Student usage guide

Your workspace brings together your thesis progress, documents, meetings, and feedback. You can see your own records and available meeting slots. Other students cannot see your submissions or bookings.

  • Sign in and set up your account
  • Share progress and thesis drafts
  • Read feedback and complete next steps
  • Book a meeting and receive a Teams invitation
  • Update your thesis and account
  • Sign out and get help

1. Sign in and set up your account

  1. Open the thesis workspace. Enter the username and initial password your supervisor provided. Use your assigned username, rather than your email address.
  2. Open Account. In Change password, enter your initial password as the current password, choose a new password of 12–128 characters, and repeat it in the confirmation field. Select Change password.
  3. In Meeting invitations, enter an email address you can access and select Save email. Your Teams invitations will be sent there.
  4. Check your name and thesis title in Account, and your expected defence month on Overview. Ask your supervisor to correct your username or expected defence month if needed.

On a phone, use the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen. On a computer, use the menu on the left.

2. Share progress and thesis drafts

  1. Open Progress and select Share progress. You can also start from the Share progress button on Overview.
  2. Enter an update title and choose Progress update or Thesis draft. Choose Thesis draft only for your main thesis document. For a Progress update, use What are you uploading? to select Research notes, Data, Presentation, Findings and graphs, or Other.
  3. Write a few sentences about what you completed, your next steps, and any questions or problems you want to discuss.
  4. Optionally attach a PDF up to 20 MB, add an HTTPS sharing link from your personal or university drive, or provide both. Check that your supervisor has permission to open a shared link. You can also submit notes without a document.
  5. Select Submit progress. Check that the update appears under Your submissions.

Share a revised main thesis PDF as a new Thesis draft submission. Your supervisor can compare it with an earlier thesis draft; progress PDFs such as presentations or data are excluded from that comparison. If an upload fails, you can use a sharing link instead. The workspace does not change permissions on your drive.

3. Read feedback and complete next steps

  1. Open a submission from Progress or Latest progress on Overview to read your supervisor’s comments and revisit its document.
  2. If your supervisor has shared PDF feedback, select View PDF feedback in the submission. Review highlights and comments, use page controls or the comment list to navigate, and select Download reviewed PDF for a copy with highlights and PDF comments. Some PDF readers show comments in a separate comments panel. Your original file stays intact.
  3. An update shows Awaiting feedback until a comment has been added, then Feedback added.
  4. Review Next steps on Overview or Your next steps on Progress. Check any due dates and mark a task complete when you have finished it.

Your supervisor may dismiss an update from their overview after reviewing it. Your submission, files, and feedback remain in the progress history.

4. Book a meeting and receive a Teams invitation

  1. Open Meetings. Select a date in the calendar; a dot marks dates with meeting slots.
  2. Select Book time beside an available slot. All times in the workspace use Europe/Tallinn, even if your device is set to another timezone.
  3. Write what you want to discuss and check the Email for your Teams invitation. Select Confirm booking.
  4. The booking reserves the slot. Your supervisor then approves it and creates the Teams meeting. Microsoft 365 sends a calendar invitation with the Teams join link to the email you supplied.
  5. Check your email and calendar for the invitation. Open the booking under Your meetings to see the agenda, invitation status, and any meeting notes your supervisor adds.

Share your progress or draft before the meeting. If you cannot attend, open the booking and select Cancel booking before it starts. If no suitable time is available, or an invitation does not arrive after approval, contact your supervisor.

Your email or calendar may display the invitation in your device’s timezone. Compare it with the Europe/Tallinn time shown in the workspace.

5. Update your thesis and account

  • Thesis title: in Account → Your thesis, edit the title and select Save thesis title. Your supervisor sees the updated title. Discuss significant changes to your thesis scope together.
  • Contact email: in Account → Meeting invitations, update your email and select Save email. Check this address when booking a meeting.
  • Full name: in Account → Profile, edit your name and select Save name.
  • Password: use Account → Change password. You need your current password and must enter the new password twice.
  • Username and expected defence month: ask your supervisor to make corrections.

6. Sign out and get help

Refreshing the page keeps you signed in during an active session. You may need to sign in again when the session expires. Use the Sign out button beside your name when you finish on a shared device; closing the tab alone does not sign you out.

Forgot your password? Ask your supervisor to reset it. Sign in with the replacement password, then choose your own password in Account. There is no automatic email reset for these username-based accounts.

A file or update did not save? Keep a copy, check your connection, and try again. Check that a PDF is no larger than 20 MB, or use a sharing link. Contact your supervisor if the problem continues.
